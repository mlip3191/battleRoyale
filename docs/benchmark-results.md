## Known limits (D-58)

Three cases fail in the baseline and are recorded as known limits, not fixed (Mike's ruling on #304, recorded as D-58 in `docs/DECISIONS.md`). No sheet, expected range or constant was changed to make them pass, and a held-out case never changes any of them.

- **cnt-05** (tuning), Roman legionary v grizzly: the fair count is 24 against an expected 2 to 8. The legionary keeps its sourced 150 N thrust (D-54). The gap is in the engine: `weapon_melee` does not change a fight (D-57), so a gladius is a 150 N push with no edge. A blade effect is post-MVP, with loadouts (D-56).
- **cnt-04** (held-out), duck v horse: the fair count is 320 against an expected 20 to 300, about 7% outside. Reported as a fail. The duck's 30 N bite is a category estimate; a sourced one may replace it through normal sheet work, and then this is re-run.
- **doc-05** (held-out), 4 gray wolves v a horse: the expected winner is the wolves, the simulator gives the horse. The source is about wolves preying on wild horses, not a duel, and the engine models a standing fight (no chase, exhaustion or pack hunting over time). The pack rule is not adjusted from a held-out case.

None of the three is an "obvious" case, so the 80% held-out "obvious" target is unaffected.
